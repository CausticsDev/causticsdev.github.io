About

Light, as it moves through water.

Caustics is a shader pack built around the way sunlight bends and settles beneath the surface. Clean water, soft lighting and a calm, natural look.

Made for the Horizon Project

These shaders are made specifically for Horizon, the Minecraft rewrite written in Rust. They are not intended for vanilla Minecraft or other launchers.

Visit the Horizon Project

Features

What's inside

Water

Clear, reflective water with animated waves and depth.

Caustic light

Soft light patterns on the sea floor and nearby blocks.

Atmosphere

Natural skies, sunsets and gentle volumetric-style fog.

Lighting

Soft shadows, ambient light and warm torchlight.

Customizable

Tweak the look to match your style and your GPU.

Optimized

Built to take advantage of Horizon's fast Rust engine.
